# Lab 2b Grading Rubric

| Criterion | Excellent | Satisfactory | Needs improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| CSV evidence | Correct run, complete metrics, clearly identified | Usable but minor metadata issue | Missing, wrong run, or incomplete |
| Calculations | Accurate summaries and units | Minor errors that do not change conclusions | Significant errors or absent calculations |
| Charts | Clear, labeled, and analytically useful | Understandable but limited | Missing or misleading |
| Bottleneck analysis | Strong evidence-based diagnosis | Plausible with limited evidence | Unsupported or incorrect |
| Engineering recommendation | Specific and justified by measurements | General but reasonable | Unrelated to evidence |
| Reflection | Distinguishes observation from quantitative confirmation | Basic reflection | Missing or superficial |
